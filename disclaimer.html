Sketchware Pro is a community-driven development environment designed to make mobile application creation accessible to everyone, regardless of their hardware. We believe that the power to build software shouldn't be limited to those with expensive desktop computers. By providing a robust, on-device Integrated Development Environment (IDE), Sketchware Pro empowers thousands of developers to turn their ideas into functional Android applications directly from their smartphones or tablets. Originally born from the passion of the developer community, Sketchware Pro has evolved into a comprehensive ecosystem for learning and creation. While the original platform paved the way, our community-led team has worked tirelessly to ensure the environment remains compatible with modern Android standards, including support for the latest SDKs and library updates. In an era where technology moves fast, older development tools often get left behind. Sketchware Pro exists to ensure that the "mobile-first" development community continues to thrive. Our site serves as the central hub for:

  • Installing Sketchware Pro
  • Accessing the developer documentation
  • Learning how to use Sketchware Pro
Sketchware Pro is, and always will be, provided completely free of charge. Our project is fueled by the success stories of our users—students who have learned to code on their phones and gone on to pursue professional careers in tech. By lowering the barrier to entry, we are building a more diverse and inclusive future for software development. Contact us
